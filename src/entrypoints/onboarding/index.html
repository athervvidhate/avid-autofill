Welcome to Avid

Avid fills job applications from your saved details. You review everything, and Avid never submits for you. Setup takes a couple of minutes and every step can be skipped.

  • Your profile stays in this browser. It is stored locally and only goes into the pages you choose to fill.
  • Google is optional. It only powers the application tracker spreadsheet.
  • Jev is optional. It uses your own TypeSafe AI key to answer more questions from your saved answers.

Sign in with Google

Connect once and Avid creates a formatted Job applications spreadsheet and keeps it up to date. Only the application details you approve go to Google. Your profile and resume stay here.

Add your Jev key

Jev picks the right saved answer for questions Avid's built-in matching leaves blank. It uses your own TypeSafe AI key and credits. How Jev works.

The key is stored in this browser's extension storage, apart from your profile. It is never logged, exported, or sent anywhere except the Jev API. Checking it costs a few tokens.
