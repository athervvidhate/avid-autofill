Avid Autofill for Job Applications

A browser extension that fills job applications from a profile saved in your own browser. You review every answer before you submit.

  • Works on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS, Taleo and more, and on career pages it has not seen before.
  • Your profile and resume stay in your browser.
  • Optional: track submitted applications in a Google Sheet in your own Google account.
