Privacy policy

Avid Autofill for Job Applications ("Avid") is an open source browser extension. Last updated October 2026.

What stays on your device

Your profile, resume, saved answers and settings are stored in your browser. Avid has no server and the developer does not collect, sell or share your data. Nothing is sent to the developer unless you choose to report a problem (below).

Reporting a problem (optional)

The drawer's "Report a problem" button builds a file from the current page: its HTML with your saved profile details, anything you typed into the form, hidden form values and email addresses removed, plus the extension version, the site name, the page address without its query string, your browser version and the labels and outcomes of the last fill. The drawer shows you the whole file first. Avid saves it to your Downloads folder and opens a pre-addressed email (or a GitHub issue); nothing is sent until you attach the file and send the message yourself.

Jev matching (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Jev matching and enter your own TypeSafe AI key, Avid sends requests from your browser directly to api.typesafe.ai for form fields it could not answer. A request contains the text of the question and its answer options, the labels of the saved answers that might apply, and, in a second step, the saved answer selected for that question. Other profile data and your resume are not sent. Requests use your key and are handled under TypeSafe AI's own terms and privacy policy. Without a key, none of this happens.

Google account and Google Sheets (optional)

If you choose to connect Google, Avid asks for the drive.file permission, which only lets it create and edit the "Job applications" spreadsheet it makes for you, plus your email address to remember which account you used. When you approve an entry, Avid writes the company, role, job URL, location, dates, status and notes you confirmed to that spreadsheet in your Google account. Avid never reads your other Drive files and does not send page text, form answers or your resume to Google. Access tokens are kept in your browser session and removed when you disconnect.

Avid's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can disconnect Google in the extension's Connections page and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the extension deletes its local data.
